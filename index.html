About us

Karibu to Nibbana Garden

Nibbana means peace, and that is what we set out to give every guest. Just outside Eldoret town in Outspan, our gardens are a quiet escape for families, couples and teams. Stay the night, linger over a meal, cool off in the pool or bring everyone together for a celebration.
